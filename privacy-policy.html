Privacy Policy — My Bistro Story

Published by MiO Games · Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy explains what data the mobile game My Bistro Story (com.mio.bistro.merge.story.game) collects, why it is collected, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to the Android version of the game distributed by MiO Games.

1. Data we collect

DataWhyCollected by
Game progress (restaurant level, currencies, unlocked dishes and staff, settings) To save your game and restore it when you reinstall or switch device — only if you choose to sign in for cloud save Supabase (our own backend)
Email address and account identifier of the Google account you sign in with To identify which cloud save belongs to you. Only collected if you use the optional sign-in; the game is fully playable without it Supabase Auth, Google Sign-In
Anonymous usage and gameplay events, approximate country, device model, OS version, app version To understand which parts of the game are used and to balance difficulty Google Firebase Analytics
Crash reports and diagnostic logs (stack traces, device state at the time of the crash) To find and fix crashes Google Firebase Crashlytics
Push notification token To send notifications about your restaurant (for example when offline earnings are ready). You can turn notifications off in your device settings Google Firebase Cloud Messaging
Purchase confirmations for in-app purchases (transaction receipt, product identifier) To deliver the item you bought and to validate the purchase Google Play Billing, via Unity In-App Purchasing
We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or microphone/camera data. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising profiling. Payment card details are handled entirely by Google Play — they never reach us.

2. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

3. How long we keep data

Cloud save data is kept while your account exists, so that you can restore your progress. Analytics and crash data are retained according to Google Firebase's default retention periods. If you never sign in, your progress stays only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game.

4. Your rights — including deleting your data

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the data associated with your account. To request deletion of your cloud save and account, email giangnb.it@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete the account and its cloud save within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also simply play without signing in, and uninstall the game to remove all local data.

5. Third-party services

These providers process data on our behalf. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, Authentication) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play (billing, distribution) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Supabase (cloud save storage and authentication) — supabase.com/privacy
  • Unity (In-App Purchasing) — unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

6. Data transfers and security

Data may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union, by the providers listed above. Connections between the game and our backend are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and cloud save access is restricted to the signed-in account it belongs to.

7. Changes to this policy

If we add a service that changes what we collect — for example advertising or attribution — we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before that version of the game is released.
